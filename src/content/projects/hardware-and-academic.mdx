Before automotive software, I worked closer to the metal: writing drivers from the register up, designing the boards the firmware runs on, and shaping analog signals before any code sees them.

## Smart home on ATmega32 (2023)

A home-control system on an AVR ATmega32, written in C with a strict layered architecture: microcontroller drivers at the bottom, hardware-abstraction drivers in the middle, and the application on top. I wrote the drivers for digital I/O, interrupts, the ADC and timers, and on top of them for the 7-segment display, keypad, LCD and motors. Access is protected by keypad-based authentication.

## ROV switching system and in-house PCBs (2022 – 2024)

Hardware engineer on ROBO-TECH, a student underwater-robotics team. I designed and fabricated single- and double-layer PCBs in-house, with solder mask, and built modular circuits on edge connectors so boards could be swapped. For the ROV *Levi* I designed the switching system that fires the flash, laser and solenoid in sync. The team competed in MATE ROV 2023, UMVC 2023 and ARC.

## Speech-recognition front end (2022)

A number-recognition module with its own analog front end: a microphone and speaker stage built with op-amps, then spectrum analysis with the FFT and spoken-digit recognition in MATLAB.
